SERVICES

Services

I keep the service menu simple. Most students begin with a conversation, then I recommend the support that fits their timeline, goals, and application stage.

Strategy

Application Strategy

A focused session to review the applicant's background, identify themes, choose what to emphasize, and decide what each part of the application should accomplish.

Essay Coaching

Personal Statement / Statement of Purpose Coaching

Brainstorming, topic selection, narrative development, structure, draft feedback, revision, and final polish for undergraduate personal statements or graduate statements of purpose.

Supplements

Supplemental Coaching

Support for school-specific prompts: deciding which experiences to use, avoiding repetition, matching stories to prompts, and showing different dimensions of the applicant.

Reader Review

Full Application Review

A holistic review from the perspective of an admissions reader, focused on overall impression, clarity, gaps, repetition, inconsistencies, and missed opportunities.

Ongoing Support

Full Application Coaching

Flexible one-on-one support across strategy, essays, activities, CV or experience positioning, application review, and final submission review.
