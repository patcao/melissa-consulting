SERVICES

Support for the story behind the application.

Writing and editing are part of the work, but the bigger goal is helping each applicant decide what to communicate and how every component should fit together.

  • Application Strategy SessionA focused session to review the applicant's profile, identify themes, brainstorm topics, and decide what each part of the application should accomplish.
  • Personal Statement / Statement of Purpose CoachingBrainstorming, topic selection, narrative development, structure, draft feedback, revision, and final polish.
  • Supplemental Essay CoachingSupport for school-specific prompts, avoiding repetition, matching stories to questions, and showing different dimensions of the applicant.
  • Full Application ReviewA holistic review from the perspective of an admissions reader, focused on overall impression, clarity, gaps, repetition, and missed opportunities.
  • Full Application CoachingFlexible ongoing support across strategy, essays, activities, CV or experience positioning, and final submission review.
