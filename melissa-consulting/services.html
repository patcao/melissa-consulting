SERVICES

Ways to work together

Every student comes in at a different point. Some are just starting to figure out what to write; others have drafts, interview dates, or scholarship essays in progress. We can talk first, then decide what kind of support makes sense.

Main Essay

Personal Statement / Statement of Purpose Coaching

This is often where students feel the most pressure. We'll talk through your ideas, what you want the reader to understand about you, and how this essay fits with the rest of your application. I can help you move from early brainstorming to a draft that feels clear, specific, and true to your voice.

Supplements

Supplemental Essay Coaching

Supplements can be tricky because they ask you to say something new, often in very little space. I can help you think through what each school or program is really asking, which stories belong where, and how to avoid repeating the same version of yourself across every essay.

Scholarships

Scholarship Essays

Scholarship essays often ask students to connect their experiences, values, and goals. I can help you find the strongest angle, shape your response, and make sure the essay feels both personal and connected to the opportunity.

Interviews

College Interview Preparation

Interviews can feel intimidating, but they're really a chance to have a conversation about who you are and what you care about. We can do a mock interview, talk through common questions, and work on making your answers feel natural rather than rehearsed.

Full Review

Application Strategy / Full Application Review

Sometimes it helps to have someone step back and read the whole application the way an admissions reader might. I can look at what comes through clearly, where the application feels repetitive or thin, and what changes could help the pieces work together.
