HOW I WORK

  1. Understand the storyI get to know the applicant's background, experiences, goals, and what they hope admissions readers will understand.
  2. Decide what each piece should doI help clarify the role of the personal statement, supplements, activities, recommendations, and additional context.
  3. Revise with purposeI help students shape essays that are clear, specific, and still sound like themselves.
  4. Review like a readerBefore submission, I look at the whole application for coherence, gaps, repetition, and missed opportunities.
