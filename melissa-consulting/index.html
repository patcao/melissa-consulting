ADMISSIONS STRATEGY & ESSAY COACHING

Hi! I'm Melissa.

I know the college application process can be stressful, and I'm here to help. As an MIT alum and former admissions officer, I've read more than 6,000 domestic and international applications and participated in admissions committee discussions.

I also worked closely with MIT's 4,000+ global alumni interviewer network, giving me a firsthand look at how essays, extracurriculars, interviews, academics, and personal context come together to shape an admissions reader's understanding of a student.

My goal is to help each student figure out what story they want to tell, how that story connects to the rest of the application, and how to express it in a way that still sounds like them.

HOW I WORK

A thoughtful process, with Melissa personally involved.

I help students see the whole application: essays, activities, recommendations, interviews, and context.

  1. Understand the storyI get to know the applicant's background, experiences, goals, and what they hope admissions readers will understand.
  2. Clarify each componentI help define the role of the personal statement, supplements, activities, recommendations, interviews, and additional context.
  3. Revise with purposeI help students shape essays that are clear, specific, and still sound like themselves.
  4. Review like a readerBefore submission, I look at the whole application for coherence, gaps, repetition, and missed opportunities.

WHY THIS WORK MATTERS

The application process is also a chance for reflection.

Many students think they have to write what an admissions committee wants to hear. In my experience, admissions readers are trying to understand who a student is, what they care about, and what they might contribute to a campus community.

The skills students build through the application process can help them prepare not only for college, but also for life beyond it: reflection, self-knowledge, communication, and confidence in their own voice.
