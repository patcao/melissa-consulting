ABOUT MELISSA

Melissa Cao

I believe education has the power to change the trajectory of a student's life, and I have experienced that firsthand as an MIT alum and through my work in education.

Why I do this work.

That belief is a big part of why I've always been drawn to working with students. It has shaped my path from being a former MIT admissions officer to continuing to work with MIT undergraduates in a different capacity and volunteering as a mentor for high-achieving students from low-income backgrounds through Minds Matter Boston.

My passion for mentoring students also led me to earn a Master's in Educational Leadership & Policy Studies, with a concentration in Higher Education Administration.

What I've enjoyed most throughout all of these experiences is getting to know students: what they care about, what motivates them, and how their experiences have shaped them.

As a former admissions officer, I had the privilege of reading applications from students around the nation and the world. I believe a strong personal statement is rooted in a student's unique background and interests, and allows their voice to shine.

Personal statements and essays give students the rare opportunity to tell their story in their own words. I'm here to help students navigate that complex process, strengthen the application as a whole, and leave with more clarity about who they are and where they are going.
