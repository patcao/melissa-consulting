Hi, I'm Melissa.

I'm an MIT alum and former MIT admissions officer. During my years in MIT Admissions, I read thousands of applications and participated in numerous admissions committees.

That experience gave me insight into what admissions officers are looking for across an application: the essays, extracurriculars, recommendation letters, context, and how all of those pieces come together to paint a cohesive story.

I know the personal statement can feel daunting. My goal is to help students bring their stories to life through strong writing and a stronger understanding of what their application communicates overall.

I also volunteer as a mentor with Minds Matter Boston, a college access program serving high-achieving, low-income students, and helping them navigate the college process.

Professionally, I currently work with undergraduate students, advising them on academic and administrative matters in the largest department at the university. Through these experiences, I have found it deeply rewarding to work with students through the college process and watch them grow during our time together.
